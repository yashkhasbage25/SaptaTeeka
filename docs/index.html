about this edition

The Saptashati is read everywhere, but the commentarial tradition on it has been effectively out of circulation: this collection of seven tikas was last printed a century ago and is not available in print today. The scans are being transcribed page by page and marked up so that each verse sits with every commentary on it, rather than in separate volumes.

Each verse is shown as मूलम् followed by the commentaries on it, each with its own colour in the margin so the same voice can be followed down a chapter.

state of the text

12 of 22 sections are readable now. The rest are being transcribed from the scans and will appear here as they are finished; sections not yet available are listed in the sidebar but not linked.

Where the print itself is illegible the text shows ⟨…⟩ rather than quietly filling the gap. A reconstruction presented as the text is the one error a reader cannot catch.
